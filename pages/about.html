OUR STORY

A little Napoli. A lot of heart.

We believe the best things in life happen around a table. So we built a place for first dates, Friday nights, and “just one more slice.”

TWO DOUGH LOVERS. ONE BIG IDEA.

A place to feel
like a regular.

For Luca & Mia, Ciao Bello started with a simple thought: a great pizzeria should feel like the neighborhood’s living room. Honest food, familiar faces, and room for one more.

That thought became our little corner on DeGraw Street. A place where the dough takes its time, the oven runs hot, and a quick bite can turn into a long evening.

Luca & Mia

NO SHORTCUTS. JUST GOOD PIZZA.

A little patience.
A lot of amore.

Our dough rests for 48 hours, then gets stretched by hand and kissed by a roaring oven. The result? A light, airy crust with just the right char. Napoli in spirit. Your neighborhood at heart.

We keep the ingredients simple because the good stuff doesn’t need much help. San Marzano tomato. Fior di latte. Real Italian 00 flour. And a team that cares about every pie.
